# Summary

An independent design studio born at the edge between editorial sensitivity and software engineering — where design and code are not successive stages, but a single language. Built to serve organizations that value aesthetic prestige but suffer from digital obsolescence.

# Gallery

A visual system built under the concept of Material Friction — the deliberate clash between the organic fragility of nature and the immutable precision of hardware. Botanical compositions intervened by mechanical keyboards, processor pins, and CRT scanlines; every photograph processed through bitmap halftones and analog scanner grain.

# Process

## 01. Positioning

Deep industry benchmark analyzing Pentagram's institutional passivity vs. Locomotive's interactive-first approach. The opportunity: clinical serenity and documentary permanence against a market saturated with loud, colorful agency promises.

## 02. Identity & Voice

Built a brand voice that reads like a well-ordered library: the 'p.d.' concept (a postscript that appears precise, intimate, and definitive), technical archive syntax in all communications, and the central thesis — beauty is not ornament, it's the structure that holds everything.

## 03. UX/UI Architecture

Exposed 1px brutalist grid, indexed image loading (bitmap reveal before final render), structural microcopy replacing generic CTAs with rational commands aligned to the brand. Motion designed as a 'defective scanner' — mechanical, precise, with analog personality.

## 04. B2B Go-To-Market

Designed the Obsolescence Audit as a Trojan Horse entry product: a technical-heuristic diagnostic report (UX/UI, Core Web Vitals, WCAG 2.2 AA) that proves value with data before selling any service. Golden rule: never sell development without designing the experience first.
