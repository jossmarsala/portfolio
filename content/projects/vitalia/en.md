# Summary

A personalized wellness platform that organizes physical, mental, and nutritional resources and recommends content based on each person's profile, preferences, and goals.

# Gallery

Visual identity built to feel calm and approachable — clear hierarchy, soft gradients, accessible layouts, and voice-guided exercise routines for a genuinely inclusive wellness experience.

# Process

## 01. Know yourself

User creates an account and completes a form about habits, preferences, goals, and declared needs — the foundation for every recommendation.

## 02. Find a starting point

A profile-based scoring engine processes the data and selects relevant content, reducing the burden of knowing where to begin.

## 03. Your personal space

"Mi Espacio" surfaces curated recommendations — exercises, meditations, meal plans — so users don't have to rebuild their search on every visit.

## 04. Explore and return

Users can follow suggestions, browse the blog, search by interest, or regenerate recommendations whenever they need fresh options.
